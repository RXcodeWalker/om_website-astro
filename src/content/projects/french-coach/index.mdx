French Coach grew out of a classroom observation: students studying French for years could barely speak it under pressure, because speaking time per class averaged about sixty seconds each. The app puts speaking at the centre — record an answer, get feedback on grammar and vocabulary, and build a session history that actually remembers where you struggle.

The core loop is deliberately narrow: role-play and topic-conversation scenarios modelled on the IGCSE Paper 3 structure, feedback generated from the transcript rather than from a chat interface, and a per-category mastery tracker that surfaces the patterns a teacher would notice across months.

Building it has meant working through speech recognition reliability, LLM feedback latency, and the hardest part — making the memory system useful rather than noisy.
